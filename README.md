# 📋 LINK.exe Attendance and Inventory Tracker

## 🧩 About LINK.exe
**LINK.exe** is a special interest group within the **College of Information, Communications, and Technology (CICT)** of **West Visayas State University**.  
It provides a platform for CICT students to explore and apply ICT in diverse fields.  

Through various events and initiatives, the organization promotes **multidisciplinary collaboration, creativity, and skill development** among CICTzens.  

LINK.exe works collaboratively with event organizers during college, university, and national-wide activities, focusing on **media production, graphics, video editing, and live streaming**.  
Our expertise ensures multimedia-driven events are executed smoothly, visual content is engaging, and high-quality productions are delivered.  

The organization also conducts **community extension projects** that bridge the gap between technology and education.  
LINK.exe was recognized as one of the **Ten Accomplished Youth Organizations of Batch 2010** for its BYTE Project.  

---

## 🎯 Purpose of the System
The **LINK.exe Attendance and Inventory Tracker System** was created to help the organization **track member attendance** and **monitor multimedia equipment** efficiently.  
This system ensures that duties are properly recorded and that equipment is accounted for, reducing loss and maintaining transparency.

---

## ⚙️ Features
- Admin-only access  
- Attendance recording for members  
- Equipment list with item details  
- Borrow and return tracking  
- Simple report viewing  

---

## 🖥️ How to Use
1. Log in as **Admin**.  
2. Go to the **Attendance** section to record member presence during duties or events.  
3. Go to the **Inventory** section to add, update, or track multimedia equipment.  
4. View or generate simple reports for documentation and accountability.
